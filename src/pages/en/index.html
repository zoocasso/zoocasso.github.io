Research

All research

I rearrange and reconstruct lens array images with geometric optics alone, without training. Master's program at Kyushu Institute of Technology, advised by Prof. Min-Chul Lee.

  1. ICMV 2026 Best Poster Award
    ICCAS 2026 accepted

    RIM and iRIM: turning angular samples into spatial resolution

    RIM rearranges the pixels of a lens array image one to one, without interpolation, into views; the lab above runs it. Its inverse, iRIM, synthesizes the elemental image of any virtual lens array from multi-view images.

    +2.39 dB PSNR and +0.14 SSIM on 7 Stanford Lytro scenes (as reported in the paper)
  2. ITC-CSCC 2025, 2026
    Code public

    Origin point optimization: sharper and faster volumetric reconstruction

    Shifting elemental images by whole pixels causes quantization error. The 2025 paper reduced it by optimizing the origin point; the 2026 paper computes that origin directly from camera parameters instead of searching for it. Image quality (SSIM) is the same as the search.

    CPU {{f:origin_cpu}}, GPU {{f:origin_gpu}}
