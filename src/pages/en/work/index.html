    XR hackathon, led a team of five

    3DOS: real-time vision enhancement on an XR headset

    I led a team of five that implemented Fourier peplography (fog and smoke removal) and photon counting (low-light recovery) in C++ and OpenCV. We built them as an Android native plugin (arm64), loaded it from a Unity app on the Metalens 2 headset, and processed only the headset's own camera, in real time.
