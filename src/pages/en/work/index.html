3DOS · vision enhancement on an XR headsetNov 2024 · XR Device Content Makerthon finalsAt the finals of the 2024 XR Device Content Makerthon (Pangyo Metaverse Hub), team 3DOS was registered with five members of my undergraduate lab, and I handled development, the presentation and the stage demo. I ported Fourier peplography (fog and smoke removal) and photon counting (low-light recovery), methods published in Prof. Myungjin Cho's research, from the lab's MATLAB code to C++ and OpenCV (one teammate helped with part of the port), built them as an Android native plugin (arm64), and ran them in a Unity app on the Metalens 2 headset on its own camera feed, in front of the audience. The slides are public.
