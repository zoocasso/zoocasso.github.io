Research

I rearrange and reconstruct lens array images with geometric optics alone, without training. Master's program in Creative Informatics, Graduate School of Computer Science and Systems Engineering, Kyushu Institute of Technology, advised by Prof. Min-Chul Lee. {{f:papers}}.

Topics

  1. ICMV 2026 Best Poster Award
    ICCAS 2026 accepted

    RIM and iRIM: turning angular samples into spatial resolution

    RIM rearranges the pixels of a lens array image one to one, without interpolation, into views; iRIM runs it backwards to synthesize the elemental image of a virtual lens array from multi-view images.

    +2.39 dB PSNR and +0.14 SSIM on 7 Stanford Lytro scenes (as reported in the paper)
  2. ITC-CSCC 2025, 2026
    Code public

    Origin point optimization: sharper, then faster volumetric reconstruction

    Optimizing the origin point reduces the quantization error of volumetric computational reconstruction, and the origin is then computed from camera parameters without a search.

    CPU {{f:origin_cpu}}, same image quality
