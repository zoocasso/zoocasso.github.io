03 Topics

Two topics, four papers

ICMV 2026 Best Poster Award · ICCAS 2026 poster

RIM and iRIM: turning angular samples into spatial resolution

RIM rearranges the pixels of a lens array image one to one, without interpolation, into views; iRIM runs it backwards to synthesize the elemental image of a virtual lens array from multi-view images. Step 2 of the lab above is RIM.

PSNR +2.39 dB · SSIM +0.14 on 7 Lytro scenes (as reported in the paper)
ITC-CSCC 2025 poster · ITC-CSCC 2026 oral · code public

Origin point optimization: sharper, then faster volumetric reconstruction

Optimizing the origin point reduces the quantization error of volumetric computational reconstruction (VCR), and the origin is then computed from the camera parameters without an iterative search.

81 reconstructions → 1 (paper: 0.31 s; the iterative search an estimated 25.45 s)
